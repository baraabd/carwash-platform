## Exact source gaps and integrity risk

- `services/communications/prisma/schema.prisma` contains only `ServiceMarker`, `InboxMessage`, `ProbeNotification`; notifications/delivery attempts/conversations/messages are absent.
- `services/reporting/prisma/schema.prisma` contains only marker, Inbox and `ProbeProjection`; product reconciliation/checkpoints/audit projections are absent.
- Both service application modules declare `BUSINESS_READY = false`; `apps/admin-web/src/index.ts` is foundation-only technical boot. These source observations do not establish accepted business provider/client runtimes.
- Both `services/reporting/src/inbox/prisma-inbox.store.ts` and `services/communications/src/inbox/prisma-inbox.store.ts` catch errors from the entire local transaction and turn every `P2002`/`23505` into `DUPLICATE`, without checking the violating constraint or rereading the winning event/hash. This can ACK same-ID/different-hash races as duplicates and ACK unrelated effect constraint failures after rollback.
- `packages/platform-messaging/src/inbox-consumer.ts` dead-letters `CONFLICT` but ACKs `DUPLICATE`. Existing `tests/integration/outbox-inbox.test.mjs` Case C2 applies the original before altered replay; it does not prove simultaneous conflicting identity or unrelated constraint handling. D04-RHASH/CHASH cases are therefore distinct required real-database/broker gaps.
