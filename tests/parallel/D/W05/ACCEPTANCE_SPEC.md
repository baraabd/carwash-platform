## INT-D-01 and current evidence limitations

INT-D-01 remains a **static source finding, not a reproduced database failure**. Both D `src/inbox/prisma-inbox.store.ts` implementations catch errors across their entire transaction and map every Prisma `P2002` or PostgreSQL `23505` to `DUPLICATE`, without verifying the specific constraint or rereading winning event/hash. `packages/platform-messaging/src/inbox-consumer.ts` dead-letters `CONFLICT` but ACKs `DUPLICATE`. That creates a plausible conflicting-byte race and unrelated-effect rollback/event-loss risk. Existing integration Case C2 applies the original before altered replay; it does not prove simultaneous identity conflict. The six RHASH/CHASH families are therefore distinct real-storage/broker requirements, with same-bytes controls.

Observation schemas are still foundation-only: Communications has `ServiceMarker`, `InboxMessage`, `ProbeNotification`; Reporting has `ServiceMarker`, `InboxMessage`, `ProbeProjection`. Their probes do not establish financial projections, product attempts/messages, consumer checkpoints or real sensitive-audit/export behavior. A historical foundation green run cannot close this file's product cases.
