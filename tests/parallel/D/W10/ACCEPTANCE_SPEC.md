## Gap guard and evidence invalidation

The coverage record is an action-level mapping, not a new wire API. Its required fields are matrix row/action/state/example identifier; screen and SCREEN/shared/prior case refs; actual owner and accepted contract/client; current actor/object/purpose scope; approved design/copy ref; source/candidate/config/artifact/environment; exact executed command/exit and redacted output; independent persisted/reconciled result; status/failure/blocker owner and artifact link. Common controls and all 16 modal fields have their own entries. A missing field or required example remains BLOCKED/NOT_RUN or actual FAILED when eventually executed; it cannot be marked PASS by a parent screen count.

For every required action compare the advertised UI and route to actual owner record/event, durable receipt/revision, financial/provider truth and operational observation. A disabled required control, synthetic live marker, arbitrary metric, simulated save, mock-only provider, placeholder success/status, permanently pending unsupported action or silent scope removal is a release gap even if cash booking works. Every accepted scope change needs explicit owner approval and traceable revised matrix; the deadline alone changes nothing.

Maintain an impact record when source/dependency/client/configuration/schema/migration/worker/image/auth/retention/design/profile/environment changes. Identify all invalidated action/example/permission/visual/provider/recovery artifacts and the exact required reruns. E reconstructs latest-target plus head, preserves all mandatory CI/scanners and frontend-specific gates, obtains independent review, verifies refs unchanged and verifies actual resulting target. Old green evidence, undocumented unaffected assertions or a metadata-only publication cannot establish missing product acceptance.
