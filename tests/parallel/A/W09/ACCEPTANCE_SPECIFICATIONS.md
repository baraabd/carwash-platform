## Limits and sequencing

T01..T04 precede actual data/device/fault work. Real providers are accepted
before their customer consumers; full affected journeys precede consumer merge.
Faults/restores use E's serialized slot and named operational participants.
D01/D02/D03 and required contrast/English/feature gaps remain open; inherited
source reproducers do not close T11/T13 or real-device families.

Run canonical Linux visuals separately from physical device and Windows
interaction. Preserve original references, all mandatory CI, assertions and
zero unapproved pixel tolerance. A framework build, marker migration, successful
foundation broker suite, emulator or source affordance cannot close this parent.

The task is NOT_DONE until every required expansion passes on a reproducible
staging candidate and operational recovery evidence is complete. Failed cleanup,
missing provider/hardware/locale/feature/restore or changed source/environment
invalidates the applicable result and remains explicit NO_GO / BLOCKED.
