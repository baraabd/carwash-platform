## 4. Genuine funds, allocation and refund evidence

- B requires independently acquired authoritative evidence bound to approved receiving merchant/account, provider transaction identity, credit direction, exact accepted amount/currency, provider occurrence/observation time and documented finality. A customer's proof/reference is a hint, not that evidence.
- Maintain independently established received merchant credit separately from allocation to an obligation, outstanding amount and unknown/disputed state (`docs/parallel/B/W04/W05_CONTRACT_PACKET.md:138–157`). Wrong amount/currency/reference or unknown Booking may block allocation without erasing genuine received funds.
- Wrong recipient or unproven signature/finality cannot establish credit received by this business. Neither reviewer decision, clean Media upload, QR display, redirect nor timer proves money. Review rejection does not erase independently established credit.
- B alone decides accounting classification/allocation under approved policy. No frontend auto-allocation, invented suspense account, exchange conversion or automatic refund. An unallocated external identity cannot be credited/allocated twice or release Work.
- Refund submission/reservation/timeout is separate from provider-confirmed reversal. Preserve original posting and linked immutable reversal, unique compensation/provider operation IDs and cumulative allowance; ambiguous outcomes retain reservations until independent resolution.
- Authorized cash refund requires actual disbursement evidence and current holder/company/custody authority. It cannot erase original receipt, assume electronic API refund, or settle treasury/custody from customer confirmation.
- Late/duplicate/out-of-order credit after cancellation/hold expiry remains financial truth, with accepted reference-only Booking notification/compensation. Billing cannot restore capacity; new reservation requires C's separately authorized operation.
