## Accepted seeding and evidence procedure

1. Verify all entry inputs, exact environment identity/current grants and one
   heavy slot. Owners approve field classification and dataset bounds.
2. Seed through accepted owner APIs/commands with original operation identities.
   A creates only its own records; other owners create theirs. No direct staging
   business-table edits manufacture a passing journey.
3. Independently read and retain protected pre-fault records/revisions/receipts,
   counts/coverage and hashes, with the approved cut/checkpoint vector. Expected
   results must be independent of restored output.
4. Capture current authorization/revocation/privacy/retention checkpoints
   separately from older backups. E renews execution/incarnation fences and
   applies current masks before resumed workers, replay or private disclosure.
5. E performs only agreed isolated faults/restores. Owners reconcile their own
   effects; A proves full customer resynchronization and continued booking.
6. Retain minimal public case/source/device/verdict metadata and protected
   evidence references. Record actual timings, unresolved effects and cleanup
   failures. Restore the agreed environment before the next case.
