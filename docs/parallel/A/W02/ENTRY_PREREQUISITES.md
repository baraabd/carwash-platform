## Dependency children: bounded provider before consumer

Names and gates are to be accepted with E before coding, not additional sprints automatically started by this proposal.

1. E accepts Identity guest and Gateway/shared contract prerequisites; define how account/guest principals resolve to distinct Customer IDs.
2. A Customer provider: its own append-only schema/migration, real PostgreSQL/restart/upgrade/constraints, owner authorization, revision CAS and transactional idempotency. Exact-purpose operational lookup and booking beneficiary resolution belong to this provider.
3. A Vehicle provider: its own DB/schema/ownership rows; accepted Customer/Identity resolution; one-time optional-plate booking description remains possible without reusable save. No peer table reads.
4. A Geo provider: its own versioned zone/resolution model, approved actual geography and real allowed/outside/boundary tests; absence of approved dataset remains fail-closed, not a sample fallback.
5. E publishes/accepts compatible owner client/route outputs at the relevant provider barrier.
6. A customer app consumer and D narrow admin consumer are accepted against already merged real providers with complete affected browser/authorization/durability journeys before their PRs merge. Fixture-only provider tests cannot close integration.

Keep parent W02-A INTEGRATION_PENDING until all task-listed cases pass on the serialized combined source. No real Booking, payment, notifications, customer stored-value funding, payroll or fourth checkout method is introduced by these data foundations.
