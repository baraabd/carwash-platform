## 2. Safe payment and private proof recovery proposal

Do not collapse these B-owned facts into one frontend paid/unpaid Boolean (`docs/parallel/B/W04/W05_CONTRACT_PACKET.md:138–157`):

| Independent fact                          | Customer interpretation under accepted read/access contract                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Independently established merchant credit | Money genuinely received by the approved recipient, retaining immutable external/evidence identity.           |
| Allocation to this obligation             | Amount matched and allocated under approved policy; a received credit may remain unallocated/disputed.        |
| Outstanding obligation                    | Exact amount still owed after authoritative allocations/corrections; never inferred from an uploaded receipt. |
| Unknown/stale/unavailable                 | No affirmative claim of received, allocated, due-zero or refunded; obtain current owner outcome.              |

Wrong amount/currency/reference or unknown Booking can block **allocation** without erasing genuine received credit. Evidence for another merchant or unproven signature/finality does not establish receipt by this business. A exposes approved scoped facts; it never invents suspense accounting, automatic allocation or automatic refund. Unallocated credit cannot release Work or be allocated twice.

- Render B's immutable booking/obligation/intent binding, exact accepted Money/currency/exponent, recipient publication and merchant instruction/asset version. Browser request cannot override them. Current UI `sham` versus B draft `shamcash`, opaque versus numeric revisions and proof intent binding require E-published compatibility; no copied private DTO.
- Missing/unverified QR, missing instructions, expired intent, cancelled/change-pending obligation, stale/unavailable owner read or amount/currency/recipient disagreement stops transfer guidance and copy/save/enlarge of invalid instructions. Show approved recovery and current review contact. Never substitute DEMO QR, phone-derived QR or local merchant settings. Previously saved external images cannot be remotely withdrawn; state their invalidity and do not imply revocation erased them.
- Preserve approved display/enlarge/save/copy and clipboard fallback for usable instructions. Bind displayed/copy/exported details to the same immutable snapshot, revalidate after async work, and distinguish prepared download from OS save. Same-device instructions stay conditional until B/E verify provider documentation and supported platforms. Wallet return/deep link/visibility resume re-reads owner facts; navigation, redirects and elapsed timers cannot mark paid.
- Private proof lifecycle is separate reservation/upload/content checks/processing/finalization/B submission. Stable owner-issued object, submission and operation identities/revisions plus checksum/purpose bind retries. Progress or a CLEAN file is not review completion or received money. E/C must publish type/size/scanning/access/retention limits; prototype compression is not that policy.
- Upload interruption/finalization or submit timeout yields visible pending/unknown outcome and authorized lookup/replay with the **same key and payload**. Do not manufacture success, issue a new key to bypass ambiguity, duplicate attachments or erase known proof. File rejection permits safe correction; replacement/removal follows C/B immutable audit/version/retention rules and never resets paid facts. Preserve a prior accepted proof while replacement is pending where the accepted contract requires it.
- Every async asset/status/proof/change callback is bound to current principal epoch, beneficiary, order, intent/method/instruction revision, operation and request generation. Clear private preview/cache on logout/account/guest change; abort is not rollback. Reauthorize current grants/object purpose on reads/replays/refresh, expire object URLs as appropriate, and reject foreign/old callbacks rather than restoring controls.
- Current R:271 hydration checks epoch/screen/order, but not a refreshed intent/status after await; R:272–273 expand/save lack after-await context checks. R:276 preview checks sheet/order; R:277 captured-object guards and R:279 local submit do not establish principal/server revision fencing. Preserve UX, replace these local guarantees with accepted production recovery; do not call their demo guards sufficient.
- Compare revisions within each owning aggregate/operation only. Billing, Booking, Media and event/delivery revisions are independent; delayed/gapped/reversed events trigger authoritative scoped reads. An older due/review response cannot overwrite newer paid/cancelled facts. Financial status and capacity/work eligibility stay separate.

- Method change requires B/C current server eligibility and explicit accepted supersession/late-transfer policy, even without uploaded proof: no proof is not evidence of no transfer. Customer can submit evidence/cancel/change requests, never mark paid, approve proof, release work, refund or edit merchant configuration. Review resolved/rejected does not necessarily mean verified/no money.
- Late financial replies after intent/quote/hold expiry, cancellation, method change or reschedule record received money once and expose late-payment review/approved disposition. They must not resurrect old appointment, capacity, assignment or instructions. Reconcile actual owner outcome instead of deciding from response order.
- Refund review/request/reservation/submission/unknown is not returned money. Show exact owner-confirmed partial/completed amounts and unresolved remainder; failed refund needs approved next action. - The inspected customer reference and React source contain no reschedule action or screen. The task requires that action; its exact production placement/copy and alternative-slot, revised-price consent and recovery views remain unprovided. Request those decisions without redesigning unrelated screens. Repeat booking/date edit cannot be relabeled as an existing appointment change.
- Proposed reschedule consumes real C availability/hold/change operation and B current quote/financial adjustment; show explicit price delta and obtain consent to that exact version. No client amount recalculation or silent consent after price changes. Freeze stable operation/fingerprint, owner refs/revisions, deadlines and policy before implementation.
- Scheduling alone commits/replaces/releases reservations under hard constraints; C Booking durably coordinates old/new appointment, Work/Dispatch safety and B adjustments. Accepted policy must define retain-old/acquire-new/release sequencing and failure outcomes. Until owner outcome is known show pending, never claim either appointment from a browser selection.
- Last-slot contention, replacement-hold expiry, downstream financial failure or lost response must resolve to one authoritative appointment and exact posted financial convergence, or an explicit recoverable partial outcome. Reuse original operation for lookup/retry; distinct stable compensation identities survive worker/database restart. A failed adjustment cannot be locally “rolled back” or hidden by resetting the draft.
