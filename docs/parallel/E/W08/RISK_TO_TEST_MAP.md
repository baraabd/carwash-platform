## Entry and decision dependencies

The complete W07 full-scope matrix remains unaccepted. Owners must supply actual
product services, app states, persistent operation receipts and accepted provider
contracts. Approved numeric/per-method privacy/retention/recovery and accessibility
decisions, representative environment identities, fixture access and resource budgets
are required before the first campaign request. Technical defaults and prototype
limits cannot replace those approvals. Retain reference accessibility debt as a
design decision dependency; it is not a waiver for production.
